Black Gold Battlefield

Save the alliance roster, update each player’s strongest march CP, and select 20 starters for Team A or Team B.

How to run the plan

This map assumes a lower-left start. R1–R6 cover the six Oil Refineries. The Hospital and Military pairs stage near their objectives and wait for them to open.

Floaters reinforce as called, then stage near the Rare Earth Factory before each alloy spawn. Floater 1 is the primary carrier, Floater 2 is backup, and Floaters 3 and 4 protect the active carrier back to HQ.

CP is the generator’s only input for strength. The caller can adjust for march speed, survivability and attendance before sharing. RDRZ strategy guide · Z-War team guide · Packsify guide

Player roster

Names and CP are shared between teams. Each team’s starter list is saved separately in this browser. Only selected starters appear in the plan. CP accepts values such as 350M and 1.2B.
